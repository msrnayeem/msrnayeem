## 🏆 Ongoing Project: [HRSoft](https://hrsoft.cloudvenus.net) (HR Management System)
I’m building **HRSoft**, a Laravel-based HR management system that integrates features like employee management, payroll, attendance, shift management, and much more. The system includes roles and permissions, where I have implemented detailed access control using the **Spatie Permissions package**. The core modules include:

- **Employee Management**: Add, edit, and view employee details.
- **Salary Management**: Add and edit salary components, manage salary cards.
- **Leave & Attendance**: Track employee leave applications and attendance requests.
- **Shift Management**: Create and assign shifts to employees.
- **Payslips**: Generate and export payslips in **PDF** and **Excel** formats.

### 📊 Exporting Data
For ease of access and sharing, every module in the HRSoft system includes the ability to **Export to PDF** and **Export to Excel**, allowing you to download and store data in the most convenient format. Modules that support export include:

- **Payslips**: Export payslips in **PDF** and **Excel** formats for employees.
- **Employees**: Export employee records in both **PDF** and **Excel** for easy sharing and reporting.
- **Attendance**: Export attendance records to **PDF** and **Excel** for reporting purposes.
- **Leave Management**: Export leave applications and data in **PDF** and **Excel** formats for tracking and audit purposes.

### 🛠️ Additional Features

- **Holiday Management**:  
  HRSoft allows you to manage holidays effectively. You can:
  - Add, edit, and view holidays.

- **Leave Management**:  
  Employees can submit leave applications, and HR managers can:
  - Approve or reject leave requests.
  - Export leave data for individual or company-wide tracking.
  - Employees and HR staff can both **export leave records to PDF and Excel**.

- **SMS to Employees**:  
  HRSoft integrates with SMS gateways to allow HR to send SMS notifications to employees for:
  - Attendance updates.
  - Leave approvals or rejections.
  - Payslip notifications.
  - Holiday announcements.
  This ensures that employees receive instant notifications about important updates.

- **Mail to Employees**:  
  HRSoft also integrates email functionality, allowing HR managers to send important notifications via email, such as:
  - Payslip emails.
  - Holiday announcements.
  - Leave status notifications.
  This helps ensure communication with employees is streamlined and timely.
